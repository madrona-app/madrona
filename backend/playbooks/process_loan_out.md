# Send an Outgoing Loan

Use this when another institution is borrowing objects *from* you — for an exhibition they're hosting, research, conservation at a specialized lab, or any temporary custody transfer out of your care.

## Where to go in Madrona

**Collections → Transactions → Loans Out** and press **New loan**.

## The procedure phases

Similar to an incoming loan but with your obligations on the *other* side.

1. **Request received** — another institution has asked to borrow objects.
2. **Approved** — internal review done; committee has signed off; loan agreement drafted.
3. **Active** — objects are out in the borrower's care.
4. **Returned** — objects are back, condition confirmed, loan closed.

## Phase 1: request received

Record the requesting institution (as a constituent), the purpose, the intended dates, the venue(s), and each object the borrower wants.

Before approving, check each object for:

- **Condition** — can it travel? Fragile items, recently treated items, or items already on display may need to be declined.
- **Ownership** — is it fully owned by your institution? Items on your own loan from elsewhere can't usually be sub-loaned without the original lender's permission.
- **Legal status** — is it subject to NAGPRA review, sanctions, or any other restriction that would prevent loan?
- **Availability** — is it needed for an upcoming show of your own?

## Phase 2: approved

Internal sign-offs the loan needs before going out:

- **Conservator review** — the object is fit to travel, or conditions under which it can.
- **Registrar review** — packing, transport, insurance, facilities report from the borrower.
- **Curator approval** — the loan is appropriate and aligns with the collection's use.
- **Collections committee** — for high-value or culturally sensitive items, committee-level approval may be required.

Once the loan agreement is signed by both parties and the borrower's facilities report is accepted, the loan can advance to active.

## Phase 3: active

Your obligations while the objects are out:

- Periodic contact with the borrower to confirm condition
- Approval for any changes to venue, display, or handling not covered in the original agreement
- Rapid response to any incident report from the borrower
- Tracking any condition changes through your own condition report workflow when the objects return

## Phase 4: return

- File a return condition report comparing to the intake photos and condition record
- Re-shelve in the object's home location
- Update the object status back to "in collection"
- Close the loan out record
- If the borrower reports condition changes, open an incident and — if needed — a conservation treatment

## Common pitfalls

- Loaning objects that were treated within the last 6 months without a conservator's travel clearance
- Skipping the facilities report review — every borrower claims to meet your conditions; confirm on paper
- Forgetting to update the object status while out on loan; it shouldn't appear as "available" in another workflow
- Not documenting any conditions the borrower agreed to ("no direct light over 50 lux" needs to be in the agreement, not just discussed verbally)
