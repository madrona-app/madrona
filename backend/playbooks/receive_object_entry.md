# Receive an Object Entry

Use this when objects have just arrived at your institution — a delivery, a loan return, a drop-off from a donor, or any custody transfer. You're documenting the moment the objects enter your care.

An object entry is *always the first step*. Even if the objects are becoming part of the permanent collection (accession) or an incoming loan, you still want a dedicated entry record that captures the hand-off itself. That separation protects you if ownership or legal status is still being worked out.

## Where to go in Madrona

Open **Collections → Transactions → Incoming** and press **New entry**. If a specific depositor or lender is already in the system, you can start from their constituent record instead.

## What to record

1. **Entry number** — a unique identifier. Most institutions use a scheme like `YYYY-NNN` or `YYYY.NNN`. Pick one and stick with it.
2. **Entry date** — the date the objects actually arrived (not the date you're filling out the form).
3. **Depositor** — the person or organization who delivered the objects. This may differ from the legal owner.
4. **Current owner** — who legally owns the objects right now. Important for deciding who can authorize what happens next.
5. **Brief object description** — enough to tell the objects apart. Full cataloging comes later.
6. **Reason for entry** — why the objects are here: for loan, donation offer, conservation, assessment, etc. This drives the next workflow.

## Terms and conditions

Depositors must accept your terms before objects cross the threshold. Use the **Terms** section on the entry to record acceptance, the depositor's signature, and a copy of the signed agreement.

Do not move objects into permanent storage until the terms are acknowledged.

## After the entry

Depending on the reason, the next step is one of:

- **Acquisition** — the institution intends to keep the objects. Open an acquisition from the entry to start the accession workflow.
- **Loan in** — the objects are here on loan. Open a loan in record; the entry stays as provenance for the custody transfer.
- **Conservation / assessment** — leave the entry in place and hand the objects to the conservator or curator for examination.

## Common pitfalls

- Leaving the depositor empty because "they'll add it later". If something goes wrong, the entry is the only legal record of who brought the objects in.
- Conflating depositor with owner when the person delivering isn't the owner — courier companies, auction houses, heirs handling an estate.
- Not recording terms acceptance. This is mandatory. Without it, you can't advance the entry to a loan in or acquisition.
